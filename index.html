Helping businesses be recommended in AI answers.

AskQuel is a multilingual web product that examines how a business appears when customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity for a recommendation. It keeps each engine separate, records the answer and its sources, and turns that reading into a clear diagnostic.

The project follows a measure → improve → check cycle: understand whether a business is named, identify who takes its place when it is not, improve the signals behind the answer, then review the result using the same criteria. The public site is built with Astro, TypeScript and Preact, with a reusable design system and build-time checks.
