CHATGPT · GEMINI · PERPLEXITY

AI visibility · Multilingual web product

Helping businesses understand how AI recommends them.

AskQuel examines how a business appears when customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity for a recommendation. It keeps each engine separate, records the answer and its sources, and turns that reading into a clear diagnostic.

The product follows a measure, improve and check cycle. Its public site is built with Astro, TypeScript and Preact, supported by a reusable design system and build-time checks.
